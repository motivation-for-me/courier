# High-speed delivery rider animation prompt

Use `assets/logo.jpg` as the exact visual reference. Preserve the real rider, red motorcycle,
delivery box, clothing, helmet, proportions, colors, and clean isolated white background.

Create a seamless four-second side three-quarter tracking shot in which the motorcycle is clearly
travelling forward at high speed while remaining centered and fully visible.

## Required physical motion

- Rotate both wheels rapidly and continuously. Add restrained radial blur to the tires and spokes,
  but keep the wheel rims recognizable.
- Keep the rider seated on the saddle at all times. The hips must remain the body anchor.
- Animate the upper body leaning slightly forward during acceleration, then returning a few degrees
  backward as the suspension settles. This is a small natural riding motion, not standing or rocking.
- Keep both hands locked to the handlebars throughout the entire animation. Elbows may flex subtly,
  but the hands must not detach, slide, or float.
- Keep both feet and legs planted in their original riding positions.
- Add low-amplitude vertical suspension movement to the motorcycle and rider together. The wheels
  should maintain believable road contact.
- Add very subtle secondary wind motion to the jacket and delivery box fabric. Do not distort the
  rider's anatomy or make the box wobble loosely.

## Speed treatment

- Use long, thin horizontal speed lines moving from right to left behind the motorcycle.
- Add only subtle directional motion blur around the tire edges and trailing contours.
- Keep the rider, motorcycle body, helmet, delivery box, and branding sharp and stable.
- Do not add a large dark floor shadow. If grounding is needed, use only a very faint narrow contact
  shadow directly beneath the tires.

## Camera and loop

- Use a smooth side three-quarter tracking camera with the entire rider and motorcycle in frame.
- Add only tiny professional camera vibration; no handheld shaking or zoom pulsing.
- Make every animated channel return to its exact starting value after four seconds so the loop has
  no visible jump.

The final result must read immediately as a real delivery rider accelerating at speed, not as a
static image shaking, a floating cutout, or a rigid character model.
